---
name: Swiss Crimson
description: International-style restraint — off-white field, near-black
  grotesk type on a strict grid, and one crimson accent doing all the talking.
  Archivo in heavy weights for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for body. For
  quarterly reviews and anything that should read as institutional confidence.

colors:
  paper: "#F4F3EF"
  ink: "#141414"
  crimson: "#C41E2F"
  slate: "#6E6E6A"

color-aliases:
  c-bg: paper
  c-fg: ink
  c-accent: crimson
  c-muted: slate

typography:
  display:
    fontFamily: "Archivo, Helvetica, sans-serif"
    textTransform: uppercase
    fontWeight: 800
  body:
    fontFamily: "IBM Plex Sans, system-ui, sans-serif"
    fontWeight: 400

webfonts:
  - "Archivo:wght@600;800"
  - "IBM Plex Sans:wght@400;600"
---

Usage guidance: alignment does the design work — flush-left everything, hard
grid, no decoration. Crimson appears in thin rules, numerals, and nowhere
else. Generous margins; type scale jumps are large and deliberate.
