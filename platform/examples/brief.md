# Q3 Sales Report
Momentum e-bikes: quarterly performance review and outlook.
Prepared by the Revenue Team · October 2026

## Executive summary
Q3 closed at $4.2M in revenue, up 18% quarter over quarter, driven by the new commuter line and expansion into two new metro markets. Churn in the subscription fleet program fell below 3% for the first time.

> Momentum's commuter line is now the fastest-growing product in company history.

## Key metrics
- Revenue grew to $4.2M, an 18% increase over Q2
- Gross margin improved to 41% on supply-chain renegotiations
- Fleet subscriptions passed 9,000 active riders
- NPS reached 61, up from 54

## Regional performance
| Region | Revenue | QoQ growth |
| ------ | ------- | ---------- |
| West | $1.9M | +22% |
| Midwest | $1.1M | +14% |
| East | $1.2M | +16% |

## Outlook
Q4 guidance is $4.8M, assuming stable component pricing and the planned November launch of the cargo model. The main risk remains battery cell lead times, which we are hedging with a second supplier.
